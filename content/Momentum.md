**Momentum** is a part of [[Newton's Laws of Motion#Second Law|Inertia]] and relates mass and its velocity in[[ Mechanics]].

# Momentum

The momentum of an object is its mass in motion:

$$
\vec{p}=m\vec{v}
$$

Its units can be expressed as: ${}Ns{}$ or ${}kg \frac{m}{s}{}$
It can also be expressed in components.

# Impulse

The Impulse of an object is the amount of Force it experiences in a time interval. It is also the change in the object's momentum.

$$
\vec{J}=\Delta \vec{p}=\vec{F}\Delta t
$$

## Impulse-Momentum Theorem

The impulse-momentum theorem relates the impulse to the momentum

$$
\vec{F}\Delta t=\Delta (m\vec{v})
$$

# Conservation of Momentum

The conservation of momentum states that: the momentum of a system unless acted upon by an external impulse:

$$
m_{1a}v_{1a}+m_{1b}v_{1b}=m_{2a}v_{2a}+m_{2b}v_{2b}
$$

# Collisions

## Elastic Collisions

In an elastic collision, the [[Momentum#Conservation of Momentum|momentum]] and [[Energy#Conservation of Energy|kinetic energy]] of the system is conserved.

Its conservation of momentum equation is:

$$
m_{1a}v_{1a}+m_{1b}v_{1b}=m_{2a}v_{2a}+m_{2b}v_{2b}
$$

Its conservation of kinetic energy equation is:

$$
\frac{1}{2}m_{1a}v_{1a}^{2}+\frac{1}{2}m_{1b}v_{1b}^{2}=\frac{1}{2}m_{2a}v_{2a}^{2}+\frac{1}{2}m_{2b}v_{2b}^{2}
$$

If the object collides with another object at rest:

$$
m_{1a}v_{1a}+\cancelto{ 0 }{ m_{1b}v_{1b} }=m_{2a}v_{2a}+m_{2b}v_{2b}\implies m_{1a}v_{1a}=m_{2a}v_{2a}+m_{2b}v_{2b} \\ \\
\frac{1}{2}m_{1a}v_{1a}^{2}+\cancelto{ 0 }{ \frac{1}{2}m_{1b}v_{1b}^{2} }=\frac{1}{2}m_{2a}v_{2a}^{2}+\frac{1}{2}m_{2b}v_{2b}^{2}\implies \frac{1}{2}m_{1a}v_{1a}^{2}=\frac{1}{2}m_{2a}v_{2a}^{2}+\frac{1}{2}m_{2b}v_{2b}^{2}
$$

## Inelastic Collisions

In an inelastic collision, only the momentum of the system is conserved.

Its conservation of momentum equation is:

$$
m_{a}v_{1a}+m_{b}v_{1b}=(m_{a}+m_{b})v_{2}
$$

Take note of its kinetic energy:

$$
\frac{1}{2}m_{a}v_{1a}^{2}+\frac{1}{2}m_{b}v_{1b}^{2}\neq\frac{1}{2}(m_{a}+m_{b})v_{2}^{2}
$$
