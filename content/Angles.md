# Types of Angle Measurement

## Degree

A degree (${}^{\circ}{}$) is a ${}\frac{1}{360}{}$ of a complete rotation
A minute (${}'{}$) is ${}\frac{1}{60}{}$ of a degree
A second (${}''{}$) is ${}\frac{1}{60}{}$ of a minute

## Radian

${}\theta=\frac{s}{r}{}$
Where:
${}r={}$radius of the circle
${}s={}$arc length of the circle opposite to the ${}\theta{}$

${}90^{\circ}=\frac{\pi}{2}rad{}$
${}180^{\circ}=\pi rad{}$
${}360^{\circ}=2\pi rad{}$

Deg -> Rad
