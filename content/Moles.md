**Mole** is a SI unit of [[Measuring|measurement]] for amount of substance and is an important unit in [[Chemistry]].

# Avogadro's Constant

The moles is based on Avogadro's Constant (${}N_{A}{}$). It is a number or amount of particles (atoms, molecules or formula units) per mole of substance. It is again based on the (notorious) carbon-12 isotope as: a mole is the amount of atoms in 12-grams of a carbon-12 isotope.

$$
L=N_{A}=6.022\times10^{23} \ mol^{-1}
$$

A mole is a set of a substance like how there are 12 objects in a dozen.

$$
1mol=6.022\times 10^{23}\text{particles}
$$
