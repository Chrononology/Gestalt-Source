**Work and Energy** is the part of [[Mechanics]] that is used to describe forces and motion on an object.

# Work

Work is the product of the applied force and distance travelled/displacement

$$
W=Fd=Fs=F \Delta s=F \Delta d
$$

Where ${}W={}$work, ${}F={}$Force, ${}d={}$distance
The unit of work is ${}J{}$ or "Joule" /joole/ or /joe-le/

$$
1J=1Nm=1 kg \frac{m^{2}}{s_{2}}
$$

$$
W_{tot}=W_{net}\\ \\
\begin{aligned}
W_{tot}>0\qquad&\text{speed up} \\
W_{tot}<0\qquad&\text{slows down} \\
W_{tot}=0\qquad &\text{maintains the same speed}
\end{aligned}
$$

# Energy

Kinetic energy

$$
T=\frac{1}{2}mv^{2}
$$

Work-Energy Theorem

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_{f}^{2}=v_{i}^{2}+2a\Delta x & \implies mv_{f}^{2}=mv_{i}^{2}+2ma\Delta x \\
 & \implies \frac{1}{2}mv_{f}^{2}=\frac{1}{2}mv_{i}^{2}+ma\Delta x \\
 & \implies \frac{1}{2}mv_{f}^{2}-\frac{1}{2}mv_{i}^{2}=ma\Delta x \\
 & \implies KE_{f}-KE_{i}=W
\end{aligned}
$$

Gravitational potential energy

$$
U_{grav}=mgy=mgh\\
W_{grav}=mg(y_{1}-y_{2})=-\Delta U_{grav}
$$

Total mechanical energy

$$
E=T+U=\text{constant}
$$

Conservation of Mechanical energy

$$
-\Delta U=\Delta T\\
\frac{1}{2}mv^{2}_{i}+mgy_{i}=\frac{1}{2}mv^{2}_{f}+mgy_{f}\\
\frac{1}{2}mv^{2}_{i}+mgy_{i}+W_{other}=\frac{1}{2}mv^{2}_{f}+mgy_{f}
$$

Elastic Potential Energy

$$
U_{el}=\frac{1}{2}kx^{2}
$$

Where:
${}k={}$spring stiffness or force constant (${}\frac{N}{m}{}$)

Elastic Work

$$
W_{el}=-\Delta U_{el}=\frac{1}{2}kx_{2}^{2}-\frac{1}{2}kx_{1}^{2}
$$

Hooke's Law

$$
F=-kx
$$

$$
F=\int_{x_{1}}^{x_{2}}kxdx
$$

## Conservative and Non Conservative Forces

Conservative is the difference between the initial and final values of a potenial energy function given:

$$
F=- \frac{dU(x)}{dx}\\
\vec{F}=-\left( \frac{\partial U(x)}{\partial x}\hat{i}+\frac{\partial U(y)}{\partial y}\hat{j}+\frac{\partial U(z)}{\partial z}\hat{k} \right)\\
\vec{F}=-\nabla \vec{U}
$$

# Power

$$
P_{ave}=\frac{\Delta W}{\Delta t}\\
P=\lim_{ \Delta t \to 0 } \frac{\Delta W}{\Delta t}=\frac{dW}{dt}\\
P=\vec{F}\cdot \vec{v}
$$
