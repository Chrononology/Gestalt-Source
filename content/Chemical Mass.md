**Chemical Mass** is a collective name for the ways of measuring mass of atoms, molecules, and compounds.

# Atomic Mass

The **Atomic Mass** is a unit of mass that pertains to the mass of an atom. It is based on the Carbon-12 Isotope where:
${}1amu=\frac{1}{12}{}$ of a carbon-12 isotope's mass${}\approx1.6605\times 10^{-24}g{}$

Notes:
The atomic mass unit can be written as ${}amu{}$ or ${}u{}$
The atomic mass unit is also known as the dalton (${}Da{}$)

## Average Atomic Mass

The average atomic mass of an element is computed as:

$$
\text{average mass } =\sum(\text{fractional abundance}\times\text{isotopic mass})=\sum\left( \frac{\%\text{abundance}}{100\%}\times\text{isotopic mass} \right)
$$

The mass presented in the Periodic Table of Elements is the average mass of that element.

# Molecular Mass

The molecular mass (also known as Formula Mass) of a compound is equal to the sum of the atomic masses of each atom of an element present.
Example:
Molecular mass of Methanol

|    Element     | Atomic Mass | Number of Atoms | Total Mass |
| :------------: | :---------: | :-------------: | :--------: |
|       C        |  12.01 amu  |        1        | 12.01 amu  |
|       H        |  1.01 amu   |        4        |  4.04 amu  |
|       O        |  16.00 amu  |        1        | 16.00 amu  |
| Molecular Mass |             |                 | 32.05 amu  |

# Molar Mass

Molar masses uses the concept of [[Moles]]. The molar mass of a substance is equal to its atomic/molecular mass but with ${}\frac{g}{mol}{}$ as its unit instead of ${}amu{}$.

$$
\text{Atomic mass } (amu)=\text{Molar mass }(g)
$$

## Conversion To and From Molar mass

Conversion uses [[Measuring#Dimensional Analysis|dimensional analysis]].
Remember that:

$$
1 mol=6.022\times 10^{23}particles\\ \\
\text{Molar Mass }=\frac{g}{mol}=\text{Atomic or Molecular mass }(amu) 
$$

Example:
How many moles are there in 134 grams of sodium? How many atoms does it contain?

$$
134\cancel{ g }\cancel{ \text{ Na} }\times \frac{1mol\text{ Na}}{22.99\cancel{ g }\cancel{ \text{ Na} }}=5.83 mol\text{ Na}\\ \\
134\cancel{ g }\cancel{ \text{ Na} }\times \frac{1\cancel{ mol }\cancel{ \text{ Na} }}{22.99\cancel{ g }\cancel{ \text{ Na} }}\times \frac{6.022\times 10^{23}\text{ atoms of Na}}{1\cancel{ mol } \cancel{ \text{ Na} }}=3.51\times 20^{24}\text{ atoms of Na}
$$
