**Molecular Geometry** describes the shape of a [[Chemical Bonding#Covalent Bonds|molecule]] in space. It is a topic under [[Chemistry]] usually taught in [[General Chemistry]].

# VSEPR Theory

The Valence Shell Electron Pair Repulsion theory or VSEPR (ves-per) theory.

This theory is used to predict the spacial/3D shape of a molecule.

The notation ${}AB_{n}E_{m}{}$ is commonly used to denote the shape of a specific molecule.
Where:
${}B_{n}{}$ is the central atom and ${}n{}$ is the number of its bonding pairs
${}E_{m}{}$ is the number of lone pairs of the central atom.

Note that: double bonds and triple bonds are counted as a single bond.
