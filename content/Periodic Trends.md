**Periodic Trends** can be used to describe the behavior of elements in [[Chemistry]] and is taught as part of [[General Chemistry]].

# Effective Nuclear Charge

$$
Z_{eff}=Z-\sigma\\
0<\sigma<Z\\
Z_{eff}\approx Z-\text{ number of inner or core electrons}
$$

Where:
${}Z={}$amount of protons of the atom or the actual positive charge
${}\sigma={}$shielding constant
The Effective Nuclear Charge can be used to explain the trends in the periodic table
