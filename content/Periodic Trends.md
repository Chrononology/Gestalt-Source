**Periodic Trends** can be used to describe the behavior of elements in [[Chemistry]] and is taught as part of [[General Chemistry]].

Representative elements are the elements in groups IA-VIIA with incomplete s and/or p orbitals.

# Effective Nuclear Charge

$$
Z_{eff}=Z-\sigma\\
0<\sigma<Z\\
Z_{eff}\approx Z-\text{ number of inner or core electrons}
$$

Where:
${}Z={}$amount of protons of the atom or the actual positive charge
${}\sigma={}$shielding constant
The Effective Nuclear Charge can be used to explain the trends in the periodic table

# Atomic Radius

The atomic radius is defined as the half of the distance between the nuclei of two similar atoms.
The atomic radius of an element is inversely proportional to the effective nuclear charge.
Where: the higher the effective nuclear charge of an atom, the smaller its nuclear radius is.

However, the size of an element down a group increases due to the existence more energy levels

## Ionic Radius

Cations are smaller than its parent atom.
Anions are larger than its parent atom.

Isoelectric anions are larger than cations

# Ionization Energy

Ionization energy is the minimum amount of energy in ${}\frac{kJ}{mol}{}$ the removal of an atom's valence electron.

# Electron Affinity

Electron affinity is defined as the negative of the energy change when an atom accepts an electron.
It is also known as the tendency of an atom to accept an electron.

Electron affinity is proportional to ionization energy.
