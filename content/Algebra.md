**Algebra** is a Field of [[Mathematics]] that deals with numbers and variables

[[Set Theory]] and [[Arithmetic|Basic Arithmetic]] are core concepts for Algebra.

An algebraic expression is defined as a combination of symbols and numbers related together by: [[Arithmetic#Properties of Addition|Addition]], [[Arithmetic#Properties of Addition|Subtraction]], [[Arithmetic#Properties of Multiplication|Multiplication]], or [[Arithmetic#Properties of Quotients|Division]].

Algebraic expressions with _distinct_ parts connected together by a plus (+) or minus (-) is called an Algebraic sum. The distinct parts are called a "term".  The [[Set Theory#Real Numbers|Real number]] beside a term's variable is called a coefficient.

Algebraic expressions (in one variable) are usually identified by their number of terms

| No. of Terms | Identification |
|:------------:|:--------------:|
|      1       |    Monomial    |
|      2       |    Binomial    |
|      3       |   Trinomial    |
|      4+      |  Multinomial   |
Operations between algebraic expressions follow the [[Arithmetic|properties of real numbers]].

# Special Identities

$$
\begin{aligned}
& (a+b)^{2}=a^{2}+2ab+b^{2}\qquad \qquad &(a+b)^{3}=a^{3}+3a^{2}b+3ab^{2}+b^{3} \\
& (a-b)^{2}=a^{2}-2ab+b^{2} & (a-b)^{3}=a^{3}-3a^{2}b+3ab^{2}-b^{3} \\
& (a+b)(a-b)=a^{2}-b^{2} & a^{3}-b^{3}=(a-b)(a^{2}+2ab+b^{2}) \\
& (x+a)(x+b)=x^{2}+x(a+b)+ab & a^{3}+b^{3}=(a+b)(a^{2}-2ab+b^{2})
\end{aligned}
$$

# Functions and Their Graphs

_Main note: [[Functions]]_

A relation of two sets (usually set X and set Y) form a set of Ordered Pairs (x,y) such that:

$$
R=\{(x,y):x\in X, y\in Y\}
$$

A Relation is a Function (Rule) if and only if an element ${}x \in X{}$ is assigned to one and exactly only one element ${}y \in Y{}$
Functions are also known as **mapping**. They are named using letters like ${}f,g,h,\dots{}$
