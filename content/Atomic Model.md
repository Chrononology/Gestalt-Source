The **Atomic Model** is the collective effort of many scientists across ages. It is related to [[Atoms]] in a way that it is the foundation of what we know about atoms today. It is an advancement in [[Chemistry]].

# Development

## Democritus (400 BCE)

The development of the atomic model can be said to have started with [Democritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democritus). He proposed that matter can be divided into smaller and smaller parts until it reaches small tiny indivisible particles. Those particles he called "Atomos" meaning: indivisible particles.

## Antoine Lavoisier (1789)

[Antoine Lavoisier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antoine_Lavoisier) had established the law of Conservation of Mass which states:  "Matter cannot be created nor destroyed only rearranged or transformed". He backed up this law with quantitative experiments. This law then served as a foundation for the atomic theory.

## Joseph Proust (1799)

[Joseph Proust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Proust) had discovered and established the law of Definite Proportions which states: "Compounds contains fixed proportions of its elements". This law then served as another foundation for the atomic theory.

## John Dalton (1803-1808)

[John Dalton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Dalton) developed the [[Atomic Theory]] which is based on the laws and discoveries of Democritus, Antoine Lavoisier, and Joseph Proust. His atomic theory explained the behavior of chemicals. He then proposed his model of an atom as a **Billiard Ball model**.

## Joseph John Thomson (1897)

[Joseph John (J.J.) Thomson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._J._Thomson) discovered the existence of **electrons** with his Cathode-Ray Experiment. He proposed his model of an atom as a **Plum-Pudding model** where electrons are attached/embedded to a positively charged sphere.

## Ernest Rutherford, Hans Geiger, Ernest Marsden (1909-1911)

[Ernest Rutherford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernest_Rutherford) proposed the existence of a **nucleus** from the results of the Gold Foil/Geiger-Marsden Experiment conducted by [Hans Geiger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Geiger) and [Ernest Marsden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernest_Marsden) under his direction. He then proposed his **Nuclear Model** which consist of a small positively charged nucleus surrounded by empty space and with electrons going around the nucleus.

## Niels Bohr (1913)

[Niels Bohr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niels_Bohr) proposed (quantized) electron energy levels based on the Hydrogen Emission Spectrum.  His proposed atomic model was the **Bohr model** where electrons orbit a central nucleus from a specific energy level/shell.

## Louis de Broglie, Erwin Schrödinger, Werner Heisenberg (1924-1927)

[Erwin Schrödinger]() developed the [[Quantum Chemistry#Schrödinger's Wave Equation|Schrödinger Wave Equation]] (and subsequently Wave Mechanics) based on [Louis de Broglie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_de_Broglie)'s proposal that sub-atomic particles have associated waves. [Werner Heisenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Werner_Heisenberg) (and some others) developed Matrix Mechanics for modern quantum mechanics. Wave Mechanics and Matrix Mechanics helped develop the newest and most modern model of an atom: the **Quantum-Mechanical model**, sometimes called the **Electron Cloud model**. This Quantum-Mechanical model gives the probability of an electron's location in an orbital.

## James Chadwick (1932)

[James Chadwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Chadwick) discovered the existence of **neutrons** from the ${}\alpha{}$/alpha particle bombardment of beryllium experiment conducted by [Walther Bothe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walther_Bothe).
