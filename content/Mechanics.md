# Work and Energy

## Work

Work is the product of the applied force and distance travelled/displacement

$$
W=Fd=Fs=F \Delta s=F \Delta d
$$

Where ${}W={}$work, ${}F={}$Force, ${}d={}$distance
The unit of work is ${}J{}$ or "Joule" /joole/ or /joe-le/

$$
1J=1Nm=1 kg \frac{m^{2}}{s_{2}}
$$

$$
W_{tot}=W_{net}\\ \\
\begin{aligned}
W_{tot}>0\qquad&\text{speed up} \\
W_{tot}<0\qquad&\text{slows down} \\
W_{tot}=0\qquad &\text{maintains the same speed}
\end{aligned}
$$
