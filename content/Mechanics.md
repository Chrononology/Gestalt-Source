**Mechanics** is a field of [[Physics]] concerning Forces

# Units and Vectors

_Main notes: [[Units]], [[Vectors]]_
A Scalar quantity is just a quantity or just a magnitude. A Vector quantity involves magnitude **and** direction.

A common example of this is:
Distance and Displacement

They both involve length. However, distance is the total length of the travelled path while displacement is the change in position.
To describe displacement with just how far an object moves is not enough, it also needs direction.

One may travel 20m to and from a shop but their total displacement is simply ${}0{}$.

# Motion along a Straight Line (1D Kinematics)

_Main note: [[Kinematics]]_

Motion along a straight line (usually with constant acceleration) involves either the ${}x{}$-axis or the ${}y{}$-axis.

# Motion in Two or Three Dimensions (2D/3D Kinematics)

_Main note: [[Kinematics]]_

Motion in two or three dimensions involve mixing both the kinematics of the ${}x{}$- and ${}y{}$-axes. The path taken by a _projectile_ in a two dimensional plane is parabolic. This may also usually apply to projectiles travelling in three-dimensions.

# Dynamics

_Main note: [[Dynamics]]_

Dynamics aim to explain motion and describe the relation of motion and forces.

# Work and Energy

_Main note: [[Energy]]_

Work is the transferred energy when an object is displaced by force.
Energy is the capacity of something to do work.
