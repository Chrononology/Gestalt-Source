**States of Matter** is a core concept of [[Chemistry]] and [[Physics]] usually taught in [[General Chemistry]].

# Main States of Matter

## Solid

Solids have a fixed shape and volume. Its particles are closer together than Liquids or Gases.

## Liquid

Liquids have a fixed volume and its shape is dependent to its container. Its particles are farther than Solids but closer together than Gases which allows them to "slide" past each other. ~~Cats are liquid.~~

## Gas

Gases expand to fill its container so that it has its volume and shape be defined by the container. Its particles are farther than Liquids or Gases. Gases may act like Liquids but they are NOT Liquids.

# Special States of Matter

## Plasma

Plasmas are ionized gases that exist in (very) high temperatures. Atoms lose their electrons and become [[Atoms#Ions|cations]] with loose electrons.

## Quark-Gluon Plasma

Quark-Gluon Plasmas are Plasmas but hotter. Their protons and neutrons separate to their [[Elementary Particles|elementary particles]] (Quarks and Gluons).

## Bose-Einstein Condensates

Bose-Einstein Condensates are are cooler (at around absolute zero) [[Elementary Particles|elementary particles]] (Bosons) and act like a super atom.

## Fermionic Condensates

Fermionic Condensates are like Bose-Einstein Condensates but consist of Fermions acting like Bosons
