**Properties of Matter** is a core concept of [[Chemistry]] and is usually taught in [[General Chemistry]].

# Changes

Matter can undergo a physical change or chemical change.

## Physical Change

**Physical change** is more related on the state of the matter or its shape. Matter undergoing physical change does not have its composition altered.
A matter's **Physical Property** is usually tied to Physical Change. Where the physical property does not affect the matter's chemical composition.

## Chemical Change

**Chemical change** is more related on chemical reactions. Matter undergoing chemical change has its composition altered.
A matter's **Chemical Property** is usually tied to Chemical Change. Where the chemical property affects the matter's chemical composition. Or simply: the matter's reactivity.

# Properties

Properties of Matter is usually split into two categories: Intensive and Extensive Properties.

## Intensive Properties

Also known as intrusive properties, intensive properties are properties of matter that do not depend on its amount.

## Extensive Properties

Also known as extrusive properties, extensive properties are properties of matter that do depend on its amount.
