**Classification of Matter** is a core concept in [[Chemistry]] and is usually taught in [[General Chemistry]].

Matter is generally split into two categories: Pure Substances or Mixtures

# Pure Substances

## Element

Elements are the simplest form of matter. They can no longer be separated.

### Allotropes

_Not to be confused with [[Atoms#Isotopes|Isotopes]]_
Allotropes are different structural configurations of an element.
${}\ce{O_{2}}{}$ and ${}\ce{O_{3}}{}$ are allotropes of Oxygen

## Compound

Compounds are combinations of two or more different elements bonded together chemically in fixed proportions. They can be chemically separated into their component elements.

# Mixtures

## Homogeneous Mixtures

Homogeneous mixtures have a uniform composition. Would appear to only have one phase or layer.

## Heterogeneous Mixtures

Heterogeneous Mixtures have a non-uniform composition. Would appear to have multiple phases or layers.
