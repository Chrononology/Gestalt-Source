**Newton's Laws of Motion** help [[Dynamics|explain the principles]] behind force and motion.

# First Law

The first law is also known as inertia

It states that: An object at rest will remain at rest and an object in motion will remain in motion until acted upon by an external force.

It is the tendency of an object to resist changes in its velocity.

$$
\sum \vec{F}=\vec{F}_{1}+\vec{F}_{2}=\vec{F}_{1}+(-\vec{F}_{1})=0
$$

# Second Law

The second law is also known as the law of acceleration

It states that: The force acting upon an object is proportional to the product of its mass and acceleration.

$$
|\Sigma \vec{F}|=ma
$$

The Newton (N) is the unit used for force.
A newton of force (${}1{}$N) is the amount of net force that accelerates a kilogram of object by ${}1\text{ m/s}^{2}{}$

$$
1N=1kg\cdot 1 \frac{m}{s^{2}}
$$

## Net Force

$$
m_{1}a_{1}=m_{2}a_{2}\\
\frac{m_{2}}{m_{1}}=\frac{a_{1}}{a_{2}}
$$

The ratio of the masses of two objects is the inverse of the ratio of their acceleration

## Mass and Weight

Mass and weight are different but related
Mass is the amount of matter in an object
Weight is the force exerted upon the object by gravity

$$
W=mg
$$

# Third Law

The third law is also known as the law of interaction

It states that: For every action, there exists an equal and opposite reaction.

$$
\vec{F}_{AonB}=-\vec{F}_{BonA}
$$
