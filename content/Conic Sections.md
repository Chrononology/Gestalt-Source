[[Conic Sections]] are shapes made from the intersection of a plane and a cone. It is a part of Pre-[[Calculus]], [[Algebra]], and Analytic [[Geometry]].

# Circle

$$
(x-h)^{2}+(y-k)^{2}=r^{2}
$$

# Ellipse

$$
\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1
$$

# Parabola

$$
y^{2}=4ax\\
x^{2}=4ay
$$

# Hyperbola

$$
\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1
$$
