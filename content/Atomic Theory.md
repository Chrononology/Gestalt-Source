The **Atomic Theory** was pioneered by [John Dalton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Dalton). The atomic theory was used in the [[Atomic Model]] and to explain the behavior of chemicals or [[Atoms]] in a reaction.

1. Elements (Matter) are comprised of small indivisible particles called **Atoms**
2. Atoms of a given element are identical in size, mass, and properties
3. Compounds are composed of atoms of different elements. Where the ratio of those atoms present in a compound are presented as integers or simple fractions. (Law of Definite and Multiple Proportions)
4. Atoms cannot be created nor destroyed. They can only be combined, rearranged, or separated in a chemical reaction. (Law of Mass Conservation)
